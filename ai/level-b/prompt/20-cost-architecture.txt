3. CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
======================================================================

COST MEASUREMENT ≠ BILLING.

The platform must first know:

WHAT RESOURCES WERE CONSUMED?

Then:

WHO CAUSED THE CONSUMPTION?

Then:

WHAT DID IT COST?

Then optionally:

WHAT SHOULD THE CUSTOMER BE CHARGED?

Do not couple internal cost measurement directly to external billing.
