4. LEVEL B CONSTRAINT
======================================================================

This is:

LEVEL B — PREPARED DAY 1.

Day 1 does NOT require:

- dedicated infrastructure for every tenant
- enterprise data residency routing
- customer-managed encryption keys
- dedicated database clusters
- dedicated GPU pools
- complex tenant migration infrastructure.

However:

THE TENANT BOUNDARY MUST EXIST IN THE CORE DOMAIN MODEL FROM DAY 1.

Every relevant resource must have a deterministic ownership model.
