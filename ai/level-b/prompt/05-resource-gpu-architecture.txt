Day 1 should be:

SIMPLE

but:

EVOLUTION-READY.

"there is exactly one GPU"

or:

"there is exactly one model"

or:

"the model always fits on one GPU"

or:

"all workloads have identical resource requirements."

Instead establish abstractions that permit future evolution.
