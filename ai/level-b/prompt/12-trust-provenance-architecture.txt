3. CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
======================================================================

INTEGRITY ≠ PROVENANCE.

A cryptographic hash can tell us:

"this artifact has not changed."

It does NOT necessarily tell us:

"this artifact came from a trusted source."

Similarly:

SIGNATURE ≠ TRUST.

A valid signature only means:

"the holder of this signing identity signed this artifact."

The architecture must distinguish:

IDENTITY

INTEGRITY

AUTHENTICITY

PROVENANCE

AUTHORIZATION

TRUST.
