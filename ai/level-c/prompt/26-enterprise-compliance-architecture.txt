1. MOST IMPORTANT INSTRUCTION
## CLAUDE MUST CHOOSE THE COMPLIANCE ARCHITECTURE
======================================================================

This is NOT a prompt asking you to declare the platform:

"GDPR compliant"

or:

"SOC 2 compliant"

or:

"ISO 27001 compliant".

Do NOT begin with a certification.

Do NOT assume that a framework automatically applies.

Do NOT assume that every regulation applies.

Do NOT assume that every customer has identical obligations.

Claude MUST independently determine:

1. which regulatory and contractual requirements may apply
2. why they apply
3. which requirements do NOT apply
4. what assumptions affect applicability
5. what technical controls are required
6. what organizational controls are required
7. what evidence must exist
8. how evidence is generated
9. how controls are continuously monitored
10. which architecture best satisfies the requirements.

The architecture MUST be derived from:

    BUSINESS MODEL
       ↓
    PRODUCT SCOPE
       ↓
    DATA FLOWS
       ↓
    DATA CLASSIFICATION
       ↓
    JURISDICTIONS
       ↓
