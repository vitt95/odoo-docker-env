======================================================================
2. CRITICAL PRINCIPLE
======================================================================

Do NOT promise:

"the system can reproduce exactly what happened"

unless the architecture genuinely preserves enough information to do so.

LLM systems are frequently:

- non-deterministic
- provider-dependent
- version-dependent
- context-dependent
- tool-dependent
- time-dependent
- external-state-dependent.

Therefore:

REPLAYABILITY MUST BE DEFINED PRECISELY.

For every replay capability state whether it provides:

EXACT REPLAY

SEMANTIC REPLAY

LOGICAL REPLAY

APPROXIMATE REPLAY

FORENSIC RECONSTRUCTION

or:

NO REPLAY GUARANTEE.

======================================================================
6. REPLAY ≠ AUDIT
======================================================================

Using 16:

AUDIT answers:

"What happened and who did it?"

REPLAY answers:

"Can we reconstruct or execute the process again?"

These are different systems.

Do not merge them without justification.

======================================================================
7. REPLAY ≠ OBSERVABILITY
======================================================================

Using 15:

Observability tells us:

"What is happening / what happened operationally?"

Replay determines:

"Can we reconstruct the execution?"

Distributed tracing alone is not automatically replay.

======================================================================
8. REPLAY ≠ BACKUP
======================================================================

Backup restores data.

Replay reconstructs execution or state.

Do not treat them as interchangeable.

======================================================================
9. REPLAY ≠ DISASTER RECOVERY
======================================================================

Using 24:

DR restores service/state.

Replay is primarily for:

reconstruction / analysis / controlled re-execution.
