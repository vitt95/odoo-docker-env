4. CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
======================================================================

DISASTER RECOVERY ≠ HIGH AVAILABILITY.

HIGH AVAILABILITY attempts to prevent or minimize service interruption.

DISASTER RECOVERY assumes that a sufficiently severe failure WILL
occur and defines how the system is reconstructed or restored.

Do not confuse:

- redundancy
- replication
- failover
- backup
- restore
- disaster recovery.

Each solves a different problem.
