1. PRIMARY OBJECTIVE
======================================================================

Design the Identity, Authentication and Authorization Architecture.

The architecture must answer:

WHO IS THE USER?

WHO IS THE AGENT?

WHO IS THE SYSTEM?

WHO IS THE TOOL?

8. AGENT IDENTITY
======================================================================

This is CRITICAL.

Determine whether an Agent should have its own identity.

Evaluate:

A.
Agent acts entirely as the user.

B.
Agent has its own identity and operates on behalf of the user.

C.
Agent has both:

- agent identity
- delegated user identity

D.
Agent is merely an execution context and not an identity.

Compare the models.

Select the correct architecture.
